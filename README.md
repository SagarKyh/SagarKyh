### 04 / Marketing Philosophy

> Strategy before spend. Measurement before optimisation. Data before decisions.

Effective marketing goes beyond campaign execution. It requires reliable measurement, meaningful attribution and a clear connection between acquisition activity and business outcomes.

I focus on building marketing systems where important interactions can be measured, analysed and improved.
